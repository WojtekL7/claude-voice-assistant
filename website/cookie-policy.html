Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026 · draft

⚠️ Draft. This document requires legal review before publication.

1. In short

This site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics/advertising tools. We only use the browser's local storage to remember your preferences, and an IP geolocation service to pick the site language. Details below.

2. Local storage

KeyPurposeLifetime
language preferenceremember the chosen site language (PL/EN)until you clear it
theme preferenceremember light/dark modeuntil you clear it

These are technical values needed for the language/theme switch — they do not identify you and are not sent to us.

3. IP geolocation (language selection)

On your first visit the site asks the GeoJS service for your country based on your IP address, to set the language automatically (Poland → Polish, otherwise English). For this, your IP address is sent to GeoJS (geojs.io) under its own privacy policy. Your manual language choice takes precedence and is stored locally (section 2).

4. Download counting

When you download the app we record a download event (version, platform). The IP address is hashed (irreversibly) and not stored in clear — it is used only for statistics.

5. Consent and control

You can clear local storage at any time in your browser settings. If we add analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will ask for consent and update this document.
