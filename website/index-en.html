Frequently asked questions

Didn't find an answer? Write to us — contact section below.

Do I need a Claude Code account?
Yes. Vibe Coding Assistant is a "remote control" over the Claude Code tool — it launches it and adds voice, tabs and comfort. Your account and sign-in stay on your side. A wizard in the app helps you add Claude Code if you don't have it yet.
Is the app free?
Yes — the Free plan is free forever (1 agent, basic voices, limited MCP & Skills). The Pro plan unlocks unlimited agents, premium voices and removes ads.
Which systems does it run on?
macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows 10/11 and Linux (AppImage). The same feature set on every platform.
Is my data safe? Where are the keys?
Keys and accounts are kept locally on your computer (including the system keychain). The app does not host the model or your CLI. Details in the Privacy policy.
What is the Groq key for?
Only for voice dictation (speech to text). It is free and optional — reading answers aloud works without it. A guide to get the key is in the app.
Can I use multiple projects at once?
Yes — on the Pro plan you run unlimited agents, each in its own tab with its own directory, model and memory. The Free plan allows one agent.
