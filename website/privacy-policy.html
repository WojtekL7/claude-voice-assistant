1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data and the provider of the Vibe Coding Assistant application is Fulfillment Polska [full legal form], registered at [address], VAT ID [NIP] (hereinafter: "we", "Controller", "Provider").

Privacy contact: kontakt@fulfillment-polska.pl.

2. Key points in short

  • The app runs locally on your computer. Your settings and keys are stored on your side, not on our servers.
  • We do not host the AI model or your Claude Code account — that is Anthropic's tool, which you use on your own account.
  • Some features (reading aloud, dictation) send content/recordings to external services for processing — described below.
  • We do not run hidden telemetry and we do not sell your data.

3. What data we process and why

3.1 Data stored locally (on your device)

The app stores in a configuration folder on your computer, among others: settings (language, voice, theme), agent configuration, and — if you provide them — API keys (e.g. the Groq key for dictation). This data is not sent to us and stays under your control. Keys are stored locally (eventually in your operating system's keychain).

3.2 Data sent to external services (processors)

To provide certain features, content or recordings are sent to external providers:

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Reading answers aloud (TTS)Text for speech synthesisMicrosoft (edge-tts service)
Dictation (STT)Audio recording of your speechGroq (Whisper speech recognition)

Using these features is voluntary (e.g. dictation requires your own Groq key). Each provider processes data according to its own privacy policy.

3.3 Contact

If you write to us (website form or email), we process the data you provide (name, email, message) solely to respond to you.

3.4 Payments and license (Pro plan)

Once the Pro plan goes on sale, payments will be handled by an external payment provider [e.g. Stripe], and a license key will be sent for activation. This feature is currently in preparation.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — providing and running the app and its features.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — optional features you deliberately enable (dictation, TTS).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — security, updates, handling enquiries.

5. Data recipients

Recipients may only be the entities listed in sections 3.2–3.4 (feature providers, payment provider, hosting provider). We do not transfer data beyond these purposes and we do not sell it.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (e.g. Microsoft, Groq, Anthropic) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, it is based on appropriate safeguards (including standard contractual clauses).

7. Retention period

  • Local data — until you delete it (uninstalling the app / clearing the configuration).
  • Correspondence — for as long as needed to handle the matter and possibly pursue claims.
  • Update server logs — for a limited time for security purposes.

8. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, object, and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO). To exercise your rights, write to kontakt@fulfillment-polska.pl.

9. Cookies (website)

In its current version, the website uses no tracking cookies or analytics tools. If we add them, we will update this policy and — where required — ask for your consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version will always be available on this page, with the date of the last change.
